At Around Technology, I work on algorithm research for a desktop companion robot product. One focus is multimodal emotion computing from physiological signals.

Highlights:

- Designed CNN + LSTM temporal-spatial feature fusion for DEAP and WESAD datasets.
- Introduced data augmentation and mixup to improve domain generalization.
- Evaluated generalization with Leave-One-Subject-Out cross validation.
- Designed emotion-triggering experiments and collected 500+ samples from 26 subjects.
