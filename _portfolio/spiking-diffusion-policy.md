This project targets the slow inference problem of Diffusion Policy. I applied spiking neural networks to both U-Net and Transformer denoising networks and proposed a learnable channel-wise membrane threshold mechanism.

Highlights:

- Tested in Pygame and MuJoCo environments across PushT, Lift, Can, Square, and related tasks.
- Reduced estimated dynamic energy consumption by 80.36% on 45nm hardware.
- Completed Franka real-robot deployment with 96% success on Lift and over 50% on PushT.
- Responsible for core implementation, simulation/real-robot experiments, and paper writing.
